Atatürk was born Mustafa. The origin of his second name Kemal (meaning 'perfection' or 'maturity' in Arabic) is speculated; some claim that this name was given to him by his mathematics teacher, possibly "in admiration of [Atatürk's] capability and maturity";[7][8] while some others claim that his teacher gave him this name to distinguish him from himself, since his name was also Mustafa.[9][10] Andrew Mango suggests that he may have chosen the name himself as a tribute to the nationalist poet Namık Kemal.[11]

After receiving the surname Atatürk on his ID in 1934, he appeared as Kemal Atatürk, while the given name Mustafa had disappeared altogether. He used the Old Turkic name Kamâl from 1935 to 1937, which means 'fortification', 'fortress', 'army', or 'shield'.[12][13][14][15]
