In April 1909 in Constantinople, a group of soldiers began a counter-revolution (see 31 March Incident). Mustafa Kemal was instrumental in suppressing the revolt, and deposing Sultan Abdul Hamid II.[42]

In 1910, he was called to the Ottoman provinces in Albania.[43][44] At that time Isa Boletini was leading Albanian uprisings in Kosovo, and there were revolts in Albania as well.[45][46] In 1910, Mustafa Kemal met with Eqrem Vlora, the Albanian lord, politician, writer, and one of the delegates of the Albanian Declaration of Independence.[47][48]
