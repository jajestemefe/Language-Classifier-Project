In December 2021, President Recep Tayyip Erdoğan called for expanded official usage of Türkiye, saying that Türkiye "represents and expresses the culture, civilization, and values of the Turkish nation in the best way".[43] In May 2022, the Turkish government requested the United Nations and other international organizations to use Türkiye officially in English; the UN agreed.[44][45][46]
Present-day Turkey has been inhabited by modern humans since the late Paleolithic period and contains some of the world's oldest Neolithic sites.[48][49] Göbekli Tepe is close to 12,000 years old.[48] Parts of Anatolia include the Fertile Crescent, an origin of agriculture.[50] Other important Anatolian Neolithic sites include Çatalhöyük and Alaca Höyük.[51] Neolithic Anatolian farmers differed genetically from farmers in Iran and Jordan Valley.[52] These early Anatolian farmers also migrated into Europe, starting around 9,000 years ago.[52][53][54] Troy's earliest layers go back to around 4500 BC.[51]
